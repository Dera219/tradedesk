# Risk Disclosures

## Trading involves the risk of loss

All trading carries the risk of losing money, including more than you might expect. Past
performance of a stock or strategy does not predict future results. No indicator, pattern, or
system removes this risk, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling something.

## Diversification and concentration

Putting a large share of your money into a single stock concentrates your risk on that one
company's fortunes. Spreading holdings across many positions reduces the damage any one of them can
do. Concentration can produce big gains and is exactly how people also lose most of what they had.

## Only risk what you can afford to lose

Money you need for rent, tuition, or an emergency does not belong in a trading account. The
possibility of loss is real, so the capital at stake should be money whose loss would not derail
your life.

## This assistant does not give financial advice

This assistant explains how trading works — order types, costs, rules, mechanics. It does not tell
you what to buy or sell, whether a stock is a good investment, or how to allocate your money. Those
are decisions for you, ideally with a licensed financial advisor who knows your full situation.
Questions asking for a recommendation are declined by design, not by accident.
